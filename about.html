Passion for gems

I've always been interested in both rocks and making things. After decades as an engineer in the software industry, I needed to find a hobby that could sustain me through retirement. After a failed trip across state to find the perfect star cut blue topaz, I committed myself to learning the art of gem cutting.

Dozens of stones and one year later, my gem cutting journey is still unfolding as I bring some of my creations to you.

Every gemstone in the Refrakt collection is precision cut by hand in Austin, Texas. Unlike mass-produced commercial gems, each stone is individually optimized to maximize its brilliance, fire, and scintillation—the three qualities that make a gemstone come alive with light.

Handcrafted in Austin

  • Local craftsmanship — Every stone cut personally, not outsourced to overseas factories
  • Individual attention — Hours spent on each gem, not seconds on a production line
  • Thousands of designs — Access to the global library of faceting patterns, plus custom designs
  • Direct communication — Questions about any stone answered by the person who cut it
  • Traceable origin — Know exactly where and how your gemstone was transformed
