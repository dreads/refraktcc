The Scarcity in America

While the United States is the world's largest consumer of gemstones—importing roughly one-third of all colored gems globally—custom gemstone cutters are exceptionally rare. The vast majority of stones sold in America are cut overseas, primarily in India, Thailand, and Sri Lanka, where generations of artisans have perfected their craft.

Fewer than 0.1% of the world's gemstone cutters work in the USA

Where the World's Master Cutters Work

Jaipur, India

Global capital of gemstone cutting. 100,000+ workers process ~90% of the world's diamonds and centuries of colored stone expertise.

Bangkok, Thailand

Colored gemstone capital with 100,000+ trade workers. World leader in ruby and sapphire cutting.

Sri Lanka

"Island of Gems" with 2,000+ years of cutting tradition. Famous for sapphires.

Idar-Oberstein, Germany

Europe's historic center with 500+ years of tradition. Known for agate and precision work.
