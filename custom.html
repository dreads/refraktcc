Why Cutting Matters

A gemstone's cut is the only factor entirely within human control. While nature determines color, clarity, and crystal structure, the cutter determines how light interacts with the stone.

Brilliance
White light reflected back through the crown—determined by pavilion angles
Fire
Spectral colors dispersed by the stone—enhanced by crown facet angles
Scintillation
The sparkle pattern as the stone moves—created by facet size and arrangement
Light Return
Percentage of light reflected vs. leaked—the hallmark of precision cutting
